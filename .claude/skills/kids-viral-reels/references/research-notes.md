## Findings used in the skill

- Formats kids rewatch: nursery rhymes, simple animation, toy pretend play, lessons inside a story,
  recurring character series. Market leaders: CoComelon (~203M subs), Vlad and Niki (~151M),
  Kids Diana Show (~138M), Little Angel; Baby Shark Dance is the most-viewed YouTube video (~17.5B).
  Sources: air.io kids trends 2026, epidemicsound.com channel ranking.
- Talking objects/personified narrators hold attention for learning content (blog claim, unsourced numbers).
- Template spread: Nov 2026 trend of a deadpan character confronted by miniature copies of itself,
  reworked by many animators keeping timing/choreography. This is the basis for template #8 "copy me".
  Source: anijam.ai Shorts trends Nov 2026.
- Length: a vendor analysis says clips over 40s get more engagement. Hence the 30-45s default. Treat as directional.
- AI character continuity is the most common failure: lock a master character before generating scenes.
  Source: ammarmanzar.com guide.
- Policy: YouTube's 2021 quality principles for made-for-kids content; July 2026 clarification that
  repetitive, template-made content with little variation is demonetized as "inauthentic content";
  AI disclosure required; YouTube Kids limits AI content to vetted channels; growing advocacy pressure
  against AI videos for kids. Made-for-kids RPM is much lower (~$1-3, per TechTimes; less established source).
  Sources: techcrunch.com 2026-07-20, techcrunch.com 2021-10-25, emarketer.com, androidauthority.com.
